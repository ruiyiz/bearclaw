# Shared Context

Facts about the household, the tools, and the projects that every agent should
know. Maintained by the owner. Keep entries short and current, and delete what
stops being true.

## Tooling

- Which CLIs, MCP servers, and services are available, and what they are for.

## Household

- Devices, shared folders, and where things live.

## Calendar and email

- Which accounts are wired up and what each is used for.

## Projects

- Anything in flight that an agent may be asked about.

## Preferences

- Standing decisions that apply to everyone, for example the units, the
  currency, or the day a weekly digest goes out.
